1The problem

Every customer looked the same size, because only the past was visible

Two customers with identical past spend can be worth very different amounts in the future. A historical report can't tell them apart.

No churn event

Fuel customers don't cancel, they just stop coming. Churn had to be defined first.

Censored data

Most customers are still active, so their true lifetime is unknown. That needs survival analysis.

Not one population

An “Ultimate Fan” and a “Low Fuel Occasional” churn at very different rates.

2The approach

Past margin + predicted survival + discounting

CLV = Historical Customer Margin (24 months) + Net Present Value of the next 5 years.

Link

Start from SCI's unified customer view: loyalty, card and till data in one record.

Segment

Assign each customer to one of eight behavioural segments.

Build the CAR

A Customer Analytic Record of ~580 features, built in SQL in five stages.

Predict survival

A gradient-boosting survival model (Cox loss) per segment.

Value the future

Survival × expected margin, inflation-adjusted and discounted to today.

Score monthly

Re-score every customer monthly; retrain every 12–18 months.
